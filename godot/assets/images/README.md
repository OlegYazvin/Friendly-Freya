# Generated Menu Artwork

## `startup_freya_hero.png`

This project-local title-menu background was generated with OpenAI's built-in
image-generation tool on September 1, 2026. The subject reference was the
user-supplied photo `IMG20260502133036.jpg`; that source photo is not copied
into or distributed with the game.

Final art direction:

> Preserve Freya as the same black, curly-coated, three-legged Portuguese Water
> Dog, standing heroically on the right with her right hind leg absent and no
> injury. Place her at sunrise in a street-level town center that evokes
> downtown Northbrook, Illinois: low-rise red and tan brick storefronts,
> teal-green awnings, broad sidewalks and street, brick paving, black traditional
> lamps with hanging flowers, planters, and mature trees. Retain a cinematic
> teal-violet alien-invasion sky and a dark, uncluttered left safe area for menu
> controls. Use painterly stylized realism. Include no people, other animals,
> skyscrapers, readable signs, title text, UI, logos, border, or watermark.

The Northbrook setting was described from public streetscape references; no
third-party streetscape photograph is embedded in the generated asset.
